## 1. Product Vision & Algerian Market Context

**WalimApp** is an Algeria-first wedding venue (*Salle des Fêtes* / قاعة الحفلات) management application and public wedding marketplace. It is **trilingual (`ar-DZ` Arabic RTL, `fr-DZ` French LTR, and `en` English LTR)** across all three user-facing surfaces and serves three primary constituencies:

1. **Wedding Venue Owners, Managers, and Staff:** Need a fast, reliable, offline-capable operational system on **Android smartphones and Windows desktop PCs** (in Arabic, French, and English) to manage reservations, configurable booking slots, customer contracts, installment payments in integer whole Algerian Dinars (`DZD`), expenses, and financial reporting—offered in two commercial editions built from one shared Flutter codebase:
   - **WalimApp Local:** A **single-device offline perpetual edition** (running on one Android smartphone OR one Windows PC without cross-device sharing).
   - **WalimApp Connect:** A **connected offline-first edition** with tiered active-device plans (**Basic: `1` active device**; **Higher-Capacity: up to `5` active devices** at a higher price, configurable by Super-Admin) providing cloud sync, individual user accounts with venue-scoped RBAC, and a shared synchronized calendar across phones and PCs.
2. **Families & Event Organizers (Public Customers):** Need a mobile-first, trilingual (**Arabic RTL, French LTR, and English LTR**), SEO-friendly public web directory to discover wedding venues and wedding service providers across all **69 Algerian Wilayas**, check reliable slot availability, submit online booking requests, and contact venues via phone or WhatsApp.
3. **Platform Administrator (Super-Admin):** Needs a centralized, trilingual (**Arabic, French, and English**) administrative back-office to manage organizations, venue owners, perpetual licenses, configurable Connect subscription plans and device limits, marketplace listings, curated/self-serve wedding service providers, advertising placements, moderation, and audit logs.

## 2. Requirements by Product Component

### 2.1 Component 1 — WalimApp Local (Shared Android & Windows Single-Device Edition)

WalimApp Local is the **single-device offline edition** built from a single Flutter codebase (`apps/owner`) targeting both **Android smartphones** and **Windows desktops**. *Note: Multi-device team calendar sharing across phones and PCs is explicitly a WalimApp Connect capability.*

#### Venue, Hall & Slot Configuration (`REQ-LOC-01` .. `REQ-LOC-04`)
- **`REQ-LOC-01` (Venue & Hall Setup):** The owner can configure their venue profile (Arabic/French/English name, default document locale `default_locale` in `'ar' | 'fr' | 'en'`, Wilaya, Commune, address, phone, WhatsApp, optional tax/registration identifiers, optional venue logo) and one or more bookable halls/spaces (`halls`) with guest capacity and base pricing (with no arbitrary hall count limit in the schema — `DEC-02`).
- **`REQ-LOC-02` (Configurable Booking Slots):** Each hall supports owner-configurable booking slots (`hall_slots`), such as Full Day (*Journée complète* / يوم كامل / *Full Day*), Afternoon (*Après-midi* / بعد الزوال / *Afternoon*), Evening/Night (*Soirée* / سهرة / *Evening*), or custom time windows, including overnight slots crossing midnight (e.g., `19:00` to `02:00` next day).
- **`REQ-LOC-03` (Seasonal & Day-of-Week Pricing):** Halls and slots support base prices in **integer whole Algerian Dinars (`BIGINT` `DZD` / `د.ج`)** with optional seasonal or weekend/weekday pricing rules and add-on service packages (`catering`, `coffee/tea`, `security`, `decor`, `valet`, etc.).
- **`REQ-LOC-04` (Authoritative 69-Wilaya Trilingual Reference Data):** Built-in offline reference data is seeded from an authoritative, versioned, checksummed dataset (acquired and quality-verified against official Algerian *Journal Officiel (JORADP)* / ONS listings in Sprint 1.1 before use) covering all **69 official Algerian Wilayas** and their **1,541 communes** in Arabic (`name_ar`), French (`name_fr`), and English (`name_en`).

#### Reservation Calendar & Double-Booking Prevention (`REQ-LOC-05` .. `REQ-LOC-09`)
- **`REQ-LOC-05` (Multi-View Calendar):** Fast offline calendar supporting Month, Week, Day, and Agenda/List views filtered by hall and reservation status, optimized for touch navigation on Android and mouse/keyboard split-view on Windows.
- **`REQ-LOC-06` (Reservation Lifecycle):** Reservations support explicit domain statuses:
  - `confirmed`: Active booking with signed contract / deposit that blocks the hall slot.
  - `tentative`: Optional short-term owner hold (*option*) with an optional expiry date (`hold_expires_at`) that blocks the hall slot `[OPEN -> DEC-03]`.
  - `cancelled`: Cancelled booking recorded with a mandatory cancellation reason and timestamp; **does not** block the hall slot.
  - `needs_review`: Flagged during Local-to-Connect migration or multi-device Connect sync conflict when two offline devices booked overlapping slots; requires explicit owner resolution.
- **`REQ-LOC-07` (Strict Overlap Prevention):** Creating or moving a `confirmed` or `tentative` reservation must validate `[starts_at_local, ends_at_local)` against all existing active reservations for the same `hall_id` and reject any overlap (including Full-Day vs. Half-Day overlaps on the same date and overnight slots crossing midnight).
- **`REQ-LOC-08` (Event Types & Notes):** Each reservation records the customer, hall, date, slot, agreed base price (`base_price_dzd`), selected add-on services (`services_total_dzd`), discount (`discount_dzd`), computed total amount (`total_price_dzd`), guest count estimate, event type (wedding, engagement/Fatha, circumcision/Khitana, conference, other), internal notes, and actor attribution (`recorded_by_actor_name`, `recorded_by_local_actor_id`).
- **`REQ-LOC-09` (Instant Offline Search):** Search reservations and customers offline by customer name (Arabic or Latin script), phone number, reservation code, or event date meeting the measurable target in `NFR-03` (`p95 < 100ms`).

#### Customer Management & Data Minimization (`REQ-LOC-10` .. `REQ-LOC-11`)
- **`REQ-LOC-10` (Customer Directory & Optional NIN/CNI):** Store customer profile (`full_name`, primary/secondary Algerian phone numbers, Wilaya/address, notes). Collecting a customer's national ID / CNI / NIN (`national_id_number`) is **strictly optional** (collapsed by default and used only if the venue prints formal legal ID fields on rental contracts), excluded from list views and search indexes, encrypted at rest in `sqlite3mc` and `.walimbak`, and wiped upon customer anonymization.
- **`REQ-LOC-11` (Customer History):** View all current, past, and cancelled reservations, payments, and generated documents associated with a customer.

#### Financial Management, Installments, Local Actor Attribution & Expenses (`REQ-LOC-12` .. `REQ-LOC-16`)
- **`REQ-LOC-12` (Installment Payment Tracking in Whole DZD):** Track deposits (*Arrhes / العربون / Deposit*), intermediate installments (*Versements / الدفعات / Installment*), and final balance payments (*Solde / الباقي / Final Settlement*) per reservation, with automatic calculation of `amount_paid_dzd` and `balance_due_dzd` in **integer whole Algerian Dinars (`BIGINT` `DZD`)**.
- **`REQ-LOC-13` (Unconditionally Append-Only Payment Ledger & Local Actor Attribution):**
  - Recorded payments are **completely immutable**. Once saved, a `payments` row cannot have **any** column updated or be deleted (enforced by database `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` triggers in both SQLite and PostgreSQL).
  - Corrections to amount, payment method, date, or metadata require recording an explicit `refund` or `adjustment` entry linked to `parent_payment_id` with a mandatory `reason` and actor attribution.
  - In single-device Local mode, actor attribution is recorded via `local_actors` (`recorded_by_actor_name` + `recorded_by_local_actor_id`, defaulting to the seeded `Local Owner` actor or an optional named local profile under `DEC-04`), which is preserved 100% intact when migrating to Connect (`recorded_by_user_id` is additionally populated for Connect actions).
- **`REQ-LOC-14` (Payment Methods):** Support Algerian venue payment methods: Cash (*Espèces / نقدا / Cash*), CCP / BaridiMob, Bank Transfer (*Virement / تحويل بنكي / Bank Transfer*), Check (*Chèque / صك / Check*), and CIB/Edahabia (record-only in Local mode).
- **`REQ-LOC-15` (Venue Expense Tracking):** Record operational expenses (`expenses`, in integer whole `DZD`) categorized by utility, maintenance, staff/catering, municipal fees, or event-specific costs, optionally linked to a specific reservation.
- **`REQ-LOC-16` (Financial Summaries & Cashbook):** Provide daily/monthly/seasonal revenue, collected cash, outstanding receivables (`balance_due_dzd`), expenses, and net profit reports, plus CSV/PDF export.

#### Trilingual Documents & Printing (`REQ-LOC-17` .. `REQ-LOC-19`)
- **`REQ-LOC-17` (Offline PDF Generation, Arabic Shaping Proof & Metadata Tracking):** Generate professional, printable **Reservation Contracts** (*Contrat de location / عقد كراء قاعة حفلات / Venue Rental Contract*), **Payment Receipts** (*Reçu de paiement / وصل دفع / Payment Receipt*), and **Booking Summaries** 100% offline in **Arabic (`ar`, RTL)**, **French (`fr`, LTR)**, **English (`en`, LTR)**, or bilingual header layout `[OPEN -> DEC-05]`, defaulting to `venues.default_locale` with per-document language selection and recording each generated PDF's metadata, byte size, and SHA-256 hash in `generated_documents`. Sprint 1.3 begins with an explicit Arabic RTL letter-joining/shaping, mixed Arabic/Latin text, and Western numeral PDF proof-of-technology on both Android and Windows before building full templates.
- **`REQ-LOC-18` (Customizable Contract Clauses):** Allow the venue owner to configure custom house rules and contract terms (`contract_templates` in `'ar' | 'fr' | 'en'`: cancellation policy, overtime fees, damage deposit) printed on contracts.
- **`REQ-LOC-19` (Platform Output):** On Windows, support direct OS printing and saving to disk; on Android, support native print spooler and sharing PDF receipts/contracts via WhatsApp or local storage.

#### Local Backups, Restore & Perpetual Licensing (`REQ-LOC-20` .. `REQ-LOC-23`)
- **`REQ-LOC-20` (WAL-Safe Streaming Encrypted Local Backups with Independent Keys):**
  - Create manual and deterministic foreground rolling local backups (`.walimbak`) using a consistent SQLite snapshot (`VACUUM INTO`) verified with `PRAGMA integrity_check` before encryption.
  - Encrypt `.walimbak` archives using **64 KiB chunked streaming `AES-256-GCM`** (preventing OOM on large databases on low-RAM phones) with an independent random 256-bit Data Encryption Key (DEK) wrapped by a user-controlled passphrase and/or printable 256-bit Recovery Key—**never derived from the WalimApp license key** `[OPEN -> DEC-06]`.
  - Display a persistent in-app reminder banner whenever an off-device backup export (to Windows USB/external folder or Android SAF / Google Drive / WhatsApp share) is overdue (> 7 days or > 10 new bookings/payments).
- **`REQ-LOC-21` (Safe Restore Verification):** Restoring a `.walimbak` archive must verify the chunk authentication tags and run `PRAGMA integrity_check` in a temporary SQLite file before replacing the live database, while preserving a `.pre_restore` safety copy of the replaced database.
- **`REQ-LOC-22` (Offline-Capable Perpetual License & `LicenseSigner` Issuance Tool):**
  - Activate WalimApp Local via a signed `.walimlic` file verified offline via an embedded **Ed25519** public key.
  - Include a standalone operator CLI (`tools/license-issuer`) backed by a `LicenseSigner` interface (supporting non-exportable hardware/OS-keystore keys or an offline encrypted vault without private keys in Git) so the Product Owner can issue, log (`issued-licenses.jsonl`), and transfer `.walimlic` files offline.
- **`REQ-LOC-23` (Perpetual Guarantee):** Expiry of the optional maintenance date (`updates_until`) only prevents activating newly released major/feature versions compiled after `updates_until`; the activated version continues working indefinitely with zero feature degradation.

### 2.2 Component 2 — WalimApp Connect (Shared Multi-User Calendar, Tiered Device Plans, Sync & Migration)

WalimApp Connect unlocks cloud capabilities inside the **exact same Android and Windows Flutter application** while preserving full offline-first encrypted SQLite operation on every device. **Connect is the release that delivers a shared multi-user calendar across multiple phones and PCs.**

#### Individual User Accounts, Venue-Scoped RBAC & Tiered Active Device Plans (`REQ-CON-01` .. `REQ-CON-05`)
- **`REQ-CON-01` (Mandatory Individual Accounts, Decoupled User Seats & Active Device Limits):**
  - Every person using WalimApp Connect (owner, manager, receptionist, accountant, viewer) authenticates with their own **individual user account** (`users`, with per-user `preferred_locale` in `'ar' | 'fr' | 'en'`) and receives venue-scoped role-based permissions (`venue_memberships`). Sharing a single owner password across staff is prohibited.
  - **Active Device Limit (`max_active_devices`) Is Distinct from User-Seat Limit (`max_users`):**
    - **Connect Basic Plan:** Allows **1 active registered device** (`max_active_devices = 1`). Multiple staff members with individual accounts (`venue_memberships`) may sign in on that single registered device (e.g., shift receptionists on one Windows PC or phone) with full role enforcement and per-user audit attribution (`recorded_by_user_id`).
    - **Connect Higher-Capacity Plan:** Allows **up to 5 active registered devices** (`max_active_devices = 5`) at a higher price, enabling up to 5 Android smartphones and/or Windows PCs to stay concurrently registered and synchronized.
  - **Device Registration, Replacement & Revocation:** When signing in on a new physical device would exceed the subscription's `effective_max_active_devices`, the server returns `409 DEVICE_LIMIT_REACHED` with the list of active devices. An **Owner** (directly from the replacement prompt or Device Management UI) or a **Super-Admin** (in Filament) can **revoke / replace** an existing lost, broken, or retired device (`device_registrations.status = 'revoked'`), immediately freeing the device slot for the new device.
- **`REQ-CON-02` (Shared Synchronized Calendar):** All active registered devices for a venue see a shared, synchronized reservation calendar, subject to each authenticated user's organization and venue permissions.
- **`REQ-CON-03` (Venue-Scoped RBAC, Per-Venue Crypto-Erasure & Clock-Rollback-Resistant Lease):**
  - Owners can assign employees to specific venues (or all venues in the organization) with granular roles (`owner`, `manager`, `receptionist`, `accountant`, `viewer`).
  - When a user's membership or physical device is revoked/replaced on the server, all online API/sync requests are blocked immediately (`403 MEMBERSHIP_REVOKED` / `401 DEVICE_REVOKED`), and on the next reconnection the client closes and **cryptographically erases** that venue's isolated local database file (`venues/<venue_id>.db` + its `SecureCredentialStore` key) without issuing SQL `DELETE` statements against immutable tables.
  - Offline staff access is bounded by a signed **72-hour `OfflineAccessLease`** enforced against both monotonic device uptime (`elapsedRealtime` / `GetTickCount64`) and a persisted `highest_observed_utc` high-water mark so manual backward OS clock tampering in airplane mode immediately locks the venue.
- **`REQ-CON-04` (Server-Enforced Financial Privacy, Safe Partial Edits & Permission-Hash Rebuild):**
  - Employees without financial view permissions (`can_view_finance = false`) do not receive `payments` or `expenses` rows from the sync API, and reservation price columns are masked (`0` with `is_financial_masked = true`).
  - When a non-financial employee edits a permitted reservation field (e.g., `guest_count` or `notes`), the client omits financial columns and the server **actively strips/ignores financial columns** on incoming reservation updates from non-financial roles so the operational edit succeeds without overwriting server prices.
  - Any role upgrade or downgrade changes the user's `permissions_hash`, returning `CURSOR_RESET_REQUIRED` (even mid-pagination): on downgrade, the client crypto-erases and rebuilds `venues/<venue_id>.db` from the role-filtered stream (preserving unpushed non-financial outbox ops); on upgrade, it resets `last_pulled_seq = 0` to backfill historical financial records.
- **`REQ-CON-05` (Multi-Venue Switching):** Owners managing multiple venues or halls across locations (where permitted by their subscription's `max_venues`) can switch active venues (each stored in its own isolated `venues/<venue_id>.db`) or view consolidated organization performance.

#### Offline-First Synchronization, Binary Asset Sync & Conflict Handling (`REQ-CON-06` .. `REQ-CON-10`)
- **`REQ-CON-06` (Auth-First Idempotent Outbox & Binary Asset Sync):**
  - Local changes are written immediately to `venues/<venue_id>.db` and queued in `sync_outbox` ordered by a strictly monotonic local `outbox_seq` (enforcing parent-before-child dependency blocking).
  - When an outbox operation references a binary asset (`generated_documents` PDF contract/receipt or `venues` logo), the client **first** uploads the binary to `PUT /api/v1/sync/assets/{sha256_checksum}` (verified by server `byte_size` and `sha256_checksum`) before pushing the relational `sync_outbox` operation, and peers fetch missing PDFs/logos on demand (`GET /api/v1/sync/assets/{sha256_checksum}`).
  - On `/api/v1/sync/push`, the server verifies current authentication, active `device_registrations`, and active `venue_memberships` permissions **first** before checking `sync_processed_operations`, and verifies `payload_sha256` equality on retries (`422 IDEMPOTENCY_PAYLOAD_MISMATCH` if reused with a different payload).
- **`REQ-CON-07` (Client Sync State vs. Domain Status):** Reservations modified offline display a distinct client-side `pending_sync` indicator until acknowledged by the server, without altering the underlying reservation domain status (`confirmed`, `tentative`, `cancelled`, `needs_review`).
- **`REQ-CON-08` (Deterministic Conflict Resolution & `scanned_through_seq` Cursor):**
  - Server delta pulls use a per-venue cursor (`venue_sync_state.last_seq`) locked with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` at transaction commit. `/api/v1/sync/pull` scans up to 200 contiguous rows and returns `scanned_through_seq` (`MAX(seq)` of the scanned window, including rows omitted by role filtering) so non-financial clients advance cleanly past filtered financial events without stalling or skipping unscanned pages.
  - If two devices book overlapping slots while offline, the first device to sync wins the PostgreSQL `btree_gist` exclusion constraint; the second device's operation transitions to `status = 'needs_review'` with a high-priority owner alert—**never silently deleting or overwriting either booking**.
- **`REQ-CON-09` (Public Listing & Availability Management):** From the Connect app, the owner edits their public marketplace profile (with optional per-language content in Arabic, French, and/or English), photos, public pricing display rules, and controls whether public availability is published.
- **`REQ-CON-10` (Connect Plan Changes, Grandfathered Pricing & Subscription Expiry/Renewal):**
  - Active subscriptions snapshot `agreed_price_dzd`, `agreed_billing_interval`, `snapshot_max_active_devices`, `snapshot_max_venues`, and `snapshot_max_users` at term creation/renewal. Editing a plan template in Super-Admin **never** silently modifies `agreed_price_dzd` or reduces limits on an existing active subscription.
  - Upgrading a plan (e.g., `1` active device -> `5` active devices) unlocks additional device slots immediately upon recording the agreed upgrade terms. Downgrading a plan (`5` -> `1` active device) either takes effect at term renewal or prompts the Owner/Super-Admin to select which `1` device remains active (revoking the excess devices cleanly).
  - If a Connect subscription expires and passes its grace period (`[OPEN -> DEC-09]`), cloud sync and public marketplace booking requests pause (`expired_local_fallback`), while every authorized device retains offline access to its local database. Upon renewal, accumulated `sync_outbox` queues reconcile through the standard conflict-safe sync pipeline.

#### Local-to-Connect Migration (`REQ-CON-11` .. `REQ-CON-13`)
- **`REQ-CON-11` (Full Historical, Actor & Binary Asset Preservation):** Upgrading from WalimApp Local to WalimApp Connect preserves 100% of existing customers, `local_actors` attributions, halls, slots, service catalogs, contract templates (`contract_templates`), reservations, reservation status history, payment ledger entries, expenses, generated PDF documents (`generated_documents`), and the venue logo.
- **`REQ-CON-12` (Staged Upload, Content-Addressed Asset Verification & Pure-DB Promotion):** Migration creates a local `.walimbak` backup first, uploads all 12 relational entity types in resumable chunks to `migration_staging_records`, uploads all binary assets declared in `migration_assets_manifest` (`generated_document_pdf` and `venue_logo`) directly to content-addressed tenant storage (`tenants/{org_id}/venues/{venue_id}/assets/{sha256_checksum}`) verified by `byte_size` and `sha256_checksum` *before* commit, and then executes a pure PostgreSQL promotion transaction (with zero S3/R2 move operations inside the DB transaction).
- **`REQ-CON-13` (Safe Interruption & Lost-Response Commit Recovery):** If power or internet fails during upload, the local database remains untouched in Local mode. Once the client enters `local_migration_state = 'commit_in_progress'`, if the HTTP response of `/commit` is lost, re-calling `/commit` or querying `GET /api/v1/migrations/sessions/{id}` idempotently returns the stored `committed` receipt (`initial_seq`), and the client never unlocks Local mode without first checking the server session status.

### 2.3 Component 3 — Public Marketplace (Trilingual SEO Web & Online Booking Requests)

The public marketplace is a fast, mobile-responsive, server-side-rendered (SSR) web application serving Algeria in **Arabic (`ar-DZ`, RTL)**, **French (`fr-DZ`, LTR)**, and **English (`en`, LTR)**.

#### Venue Discovery, Search, Optional Per-Language Content & 69-Wilaya Filtering (`REQ-MKT-01` .. `REQ-MKT-06`)
- **`REQ-MKT-01` (Trilingual SSR, URL Prefixing & SEO):** Indexable SSR pages with clean localized URLs (`/ar/...`, `/fr/...`, `/en/...`), `hreflang="ar-DZ"`, `hreflang="fr-DZ"`, `hreflang="en"`, and `hreflang="x-default"` tags, `LocalBusiness`/`EventVenue` JSON-LD structured data, OpenGraph tags, and dynamic XML sitemaps. Visitor language preference is determined by the URL prefix, persisted in a locale cookie when switched, and negotiated from `Accept-Language` on the root `/` redirect (falling back to `/fr`).
- **`REQ-MKT-02` (69-Wilaya & Commune Search):** Browse and filter venues across all **69 Algerian Wilayas** and their 1,541 communes in Arabic, French, and English.
- **`REQ-MKT-03` (Capacity, Price, Date & Service Filters):** Filter venues by guest capacity (including separate men/women hall capacity where applicable), price range (`DZD`), available date/slot, and amenities (parking, catering, air conditioning, generator, bridal suite, accessibility).
- **`REQ-MKT-04` (Rich Venue Profile Pages, Optional Per-Language Content & Deterministic Fallback):**
  - Display verified venue descriptions, photo galleries (`WebP`/`AVIF`), halls and slot definitions, transparent pricing policy (exact price, "starting from", or "on quote" `[OPEN -> DEC-14]`), location map/directions, and direct contact actions (Phone call, WhatsApp deep-link with prefilled message in the visitor's active language).
  - **Optional Per-Language Content & Fallback:** Venue owners and service providers can enter profile titles and descriptions in **any one, two, or all three languages (`ar`, `fr`, `en`)**; only **at least one language** is required to publish. If a field is missing in the visitor's active locale `L`, the marketplace renders the available text using the deterministic fallback order (`en -> fr -> ar`, `fr -> ar -> en`, `ar -> fr -> en`) wrapped in an explicit `<bdi dir="rtl|ltr">` container while keeping all surrounding page navigation, labels, amenity badges, and CTAs in locale `L`.
- **`REQ-MKT-05` (Reliable Public Availability Calendar):** Display read-only slot availability (`Available` vs. `Booked/Unavailable`) derived from the venue's synced `confirmed` and `tentative` reservations (when venue sync is fresh)—**never exposing private customer names, phone numbers, or contract amounts**.
- **`REQ-MKT-06` (Curated Wedding Service Provider Directory):** Provide a trilingual directory of non-venue wedding providers (caterers, photographers, decorators, traditional outfits/Negafa, pastry, DJs/orchestras, car rental) searchable by category and Wilaya (staff-curated in Phase 3; provider self-service in Phase 4).

#### Online Booking Requests (`REQ-MKT-07` .. `REQ-MKT-11`)
- **`REQ-MKT-07` (Strict Separation of Requests and Reservations):** Public visitors submit **Booking Requests** (`booking_requests`), **never** direct `reservations`. A booking request specifies the target venue, hall, requested date, requested slot, requester name, phone/WhatsApp, estimated guest count, event type, preferred language (`ar`, `fr`, `en`), and optional message, plus anti-spam protection (`[OPEN -> DEC-12]`) and Law 18-07 privacy consent recording.
- **`REQ-MKT-08` (Non-Blocking Pending State):** Pending booking requests (`status = 'pending'`) do **not** block the venue's calendar slot or prevent the owner from recording a walk-in booking. Multiple requests may be submitted for a popular date until the owner approves one.
- **`REQ-MKT-09` (Owner Review & Approval Workflow):** Venue owners/managers receive booking requests in WalimApp Connect (Android/Windows) and can contact the requester via phone/WhatsApp, then **Approve** or **Reject** the request.
- **`REQ-MKT-10` (Atomic Approval & Availability Validation):** Approving a `booking_request` validates in a single PostgreSQL transaction that the requested hall and slot are still free, creates or links the `customer`, inserts a `confirmed` (or `tentative`) `reservation` guarded by the `btree_gist` exclusion constraint, and marks the request `approved` (`resulting_reservation_id`). It must **never** silently overwrite an existing reservation.
- **`REQ-MKT-11` (Competing Request Handling):** When a slot becomes booked, any other `pending` booking requests for the same hall and overlapping time window are automatically flagged in the owner UI so the owner can reject them or offer an alternative date `[OPEN -> DEC-13]`.

### 2.4 Component 4 — Super-Admin Back-Office (Filament — Trilingual `ar` / `fr` / `en`)

The Super-Admin interface provides centralized governance, commercial operations, and support in **Arabic (`ar-DZ`, RTL)**, **French (`fr-DZ`, LTR)**, and **English (`en`, LTR)**.

- **`REQ-ADM-01` (Organization, Owner & Venue Management):** Create, inspect, suspend, or verify organizations, venue owners, venues, and halls; assist with onboarding and Local-to-Connect migrations.
- **`REQ-ADM-02` (Perpetual License Issuance, Import & Device Transfers):** Import the offline operator license ledger (`issued-licenses.jsonl`), generate and sign new WalimApp Local perpetual licenses (`.walimlic`), manage maintenance windows (`updates_until`), view bound installation fingerprints, and approve hardware/device transfer requests.
- **`REQ-ADM-03` (Configurable Connect Subscription Plans, Device Limits & Billing Management):**
  - Create and configure `subscription_plans` with trilingual names (`name_ar`, `name_fr`, `name_en`), integer whole-Dinar prices (`price_dzd`), billing intervals (`monthly`, `quarterly`, `semi_annual`, `annual`, `custom_days`), **active device limits (`max_active_devices`, seeded with Basic = `1` active device and Higher-Capacity = `5` active devices)**, and applicable venue/user limits (`max_venues`, `max_users`) `[OPEN -> DEC-08 for initial commercial DZD price points]`.
  - Manage tenant `subscriptions` (with snapshotted `agreed_price_dzd` and optional per-tenant limit overrides so plan edits never silently alter agreed prices on existing subscriptions), manual payment confirmations (bank transfer / CCP / BaridiMob / cash receipt), device replacements/revocations (`device_registrations`), renewal dates, and grace periods.
- **`REQ-ADM-04` (Marketplace Moderation & Quality Control):** Review, approve, feature, or unpublish venue listings, photos, and provider profiles (including per-language content in Arabic, French, and English) before or after publication.
- **`REQ-ADM-05` (Booking Request Support & Diagnostics):** View platform-wide `booking_requests` delivery status, sync health diagnostics, and conflict/migration logs to support venue owners.
- **`REQ-ADM-06` (Advertising & Featured Placements):** Manage featured venue badges, homepage/Wilaya banners, and sponsored placements with start/end dates and Wilaya targeting (admin-managed in Phase 3; expanded self-serve in Phase 4).
- **`REQ-ADM-07` (Immutable Platform Audit Log):** Record an append-only `audit_logs` trail of all administrative actions, license operations, subscription plan/override changes, device revocations/replacements, permission modifications, and moderation decisions.
